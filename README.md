# NCET Digital Study Hub & Campus ERP Portal


## 🌟 Modules & Features Overview

### 1. Student and Faculty Login
- Role-based authentication (`Student`, `Faculty`, `Parent`, `Admin`) with automatic redirection to dedicated dashboards.
- Quick 1-click test login presets for all four roles.
- Secure session management with instant logout and quick role switching.

### 2. Student & Faculty Dashboards
- **Student Dashboard**: Real-time metrics for Overall Attendance %, Cumulative CGPA (`8.71`), Fee Balance (`₹32,000`), Upcoming Exam count, and direct shortcuts to all modules.
- **Faculty Dashboard**: Assigned course catalog, today's lecture schedule with rooms (`LH-302`, `Cloud Lab 2`), quick attendance recording, marks evaluation, and circular broadcasting.

### 3. Attendance Management
- **Faculty**: Daily attendance roster by course and date with quick "Mark All Present", individual toggles for `Present` / `Absent` / `Leave`, and local persistence.
- **Student & Parent**: Subject-wise visual percentage bars. Prominent red warning banner when attendance in any subject falls below **75%** (e.g. *Full Stack Web Architecture at 67.8%*).

### 4. Course & Elective Registration
- Core compulsory and professional elective course listings with faculty names, rooms, class schedules, and real-time seat capacities.
- Credit-limit validation (maximum 24 credits per semester).
- Register & drop actions with dynamic credit progress bar and printable/downloadable Registration Summary Slip.

### 5. Examination & Result Management
- **Exam Timetable**: Schedule of Semester End Examinations (SEE) and practicals with date, time slot, venue, and hall ticket readiness.
- **Semester Results**: Comprehensive grade sheets for Sem 5 and Sem 4 with internal IA marks (out of 50), SEE marks (out of 100), letter grades (`O`, `A+`, `A`), grade points, SGPA, and cumulative CGPA.
- **Downloadable Result Card**: Official printable NCET Autonomous Grade Card with institutional seal.
- **Faculty Marks Entry**: Interface to enter and publish IA and SEE marks.

### 6. Fee Management
- Itemized semester fee structure (Tuition, Lab & Software, Exam, Library, Campus Development).
- Paid vs Pending balance tracking with due date indicators.
- Mock **"Pay Now"** gateway simulator supporting UPI QR codes, Credit/Debit cards, and Net Banking.
- Printable, official downloadable payment receipts with transaction IDs.
- **Admin Defaulters Console**: Filterable list of students with unpaid dues and a "Send Notice" reminder trigger.

### 7. Student Grievance Portal
- Student ticket lodging interface with category selection (Academic, Examination, Fees, Hostel & Mess, Infrastructure, Anti-Ragging), subject, description, and file attachment simulator.
- Generation of unique ticket IDs (e.g. `NCET-GRV-2026-042`).
- Visual progress timeline: `Submitted` ➔ `In Review` ➔ `Resolved`.
- Administrative redressal console to change ticket status and submit official resolution responses.

### 8. Notifications & Announcements
- Campus circular publishing tool for Admin and Faculty with audience targeting (`All`, `Department`, `Course`) and priority flags (`Normal`, `Urgent`).
- Real-time unread notification bell with badge counts and alert dropdown.

### 9. Parent Dashboard
- Guardian portal with read-only access to ward's academic status.
- High-visibility warning banners for low subject attendance (< 75%) and pending fee balances.
- Complete visibility into attendance breakdown, grade cards, and college circulars.

### 10. Administrator Dashboard
- High-level KPIs: Active Students (1,480), Teaching Faculty (94), Active Courses, Fee Collection Recovery Rate (82.4%), Open Grievances.
- Campus analytics charts for student strength per department, attendance health, and fee collections.
- User directory for students and faculty with search and filter capabilities.
- Central management of courses, exams, fees, grievances, and announcements.
